A Message of Welcome & Distinction

Dr. Harleen Kaur, "We are immensely honoured to announce that Guru Nanak Foundation Global School, Urban Estate, Phase-1, Jalandhar, shall serve as the proud host institution for the inaugural chapter of GNFGS-MUN'26."

This distinguished two-day conference is meticulously scheduled to convene in the year 2026, bringing together young minds of exceptional calibre from institutions across the region. GNFGS-MUN'26 is conceived as a transformative intellectual arena — one where the leaders of tomorrow engage with the pressing geopolitical, socioeconomic, and humanitarian challenges confronting our global community today.

Through the esteemed Model United Nations framework, delegates shall be endowed with an unparalleled opportunity to refine their capacities for critical deliberation, diplomatic negotiation, and persuasive advocacy — skills that transcend the conference hall and resonate throughout a lifetime of leadership.

We extend a most cordial and enthusiastic invitation to the emerging luminaries of your distinguished institution to grace GNFGS-MUN'26 with their presence — an event architectured to be both educatively elevating and profoundly enriching.

Registration & Allotment

Secure Your Portfolio

Committee and portfolio allocations, once officially assigned, shall be considered final and non-transferable and will not be subject to any modification under any circumstances. Delegation preferences and committee requests must be submitted exclusively through the official registration form provided below in this brochure. The Organising Committee reserves the sole discretion to allocate committees and portfolios based on availability and the conference requirements.

Each participating school is required to be accompanied by one faculty advisor per delegation. Comprehensive documentation — including the conference's Rules of Procedure, committee details, country matrix, executive board composition, and the official itinerary — shall be disseminated via our official social media channels, as well as through direct briefings by our assigned coordination team.

⚑   EARLY REGISTRATION DEADLINE: To ensure allotment of preferred portfolios, delegates must register on or before 30th July, 2026.

Upon successful registration, each delegate shall be formally assigned a portfolio — comprising the nation or designated post they shall represent, the corresponding committee assignment, and the specific agenda topic for deliberation. Portfolio allotments shall be determined with due consideration of prior MUN experience.

★   Allotments shall be officially confirmed exclusively upon receipt of the requisite conference fee. Fees, once remitted, are strictly non-transferable and non-refundable.
